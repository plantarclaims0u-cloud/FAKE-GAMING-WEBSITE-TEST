Character Summary

Sigma is a brilliant astrophysicist whose mind was fractured after a failed experiment involving gravity.

He shifts between calm lucidity and instability, often speaking in fragmented thoughts.

Despite his condition, he wields immense control over gravitational forces, making him extremely dangerous.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Dr. Siebren de Kuiper was a renowned scientist studying black holes and gravitational phenomena.

An experiment went catastrophically wrong, exposing him to unknown forces that altered his mind.

He was institutionalized but later extracted by Talon, who weaponized his abilities.

Under their control, he became a powerful but unstable asset.

Fragments of his former self still struggle to understand what happened to him.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Conducted groundbreaking research in astrophysics.
  • Survived a failed gravity experiment that altered his mind.
  • Was captured and manipulated by Talon.
  • Uses gravitational powers as a living weapon.
  • Represents the danger of unchecked scientific ambition.
