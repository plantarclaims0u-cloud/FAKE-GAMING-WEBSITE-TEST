Character Summary

Kiriko is a support hero who blends traditional spiritual healing with modern-day ninja training. As the daughter of a shrine maiden and the goddaughter of a powerful clan leader, Kiriko grew up at the intersection of ancient wisdom and martial discipline. With the guidance of her grandmother, she learned spiritual techniques and how to commune with the fox spirit, while her training alongside the Shimada clan’s soldiers (same clan as Hanzo and Genji) taught her deadly precision and agility.

Now, Kiriko uses her powers to protect her hometown of Kanezaka from gangs like the Hashimoto yakuza and to heal those in need, fighting injustice on her own terms—with a blend of tradition, magic, and cutting-edge combat skills.

Backstory

Origins and Childhood in Kanezaka:

Kiriko Kamori was born and raised in Kanezaka, a historic and spiritual district near Hanamura, Japan. Her upbringing was uniquely divided between two worlds:

  • On one side, she was raised by her grandmother, a traditional shrine maiden and spiritual healer who instilled in her the ancient practices of cleansing rituals, ofuda healing, and a deep bond with the mystical fox spirit, the kitsune.
  • On the other side, her mother was a modern, strict woman who pushed Kiriko to follow her footsteps in more structured, disciplined ways—particularly in martial arts and tactical thinking.

The Kamori family protected the sacred Kanezaka Shrine for generations, where the power of the fox spirit is said to reside. Kiriko’s bond with the kitsune grew stronger over time, enhancing her healing abilities and reflexes in combat.

Recruitment into Overwatch:

Training with the Shimada Clan:

Kiriko’s godmother was connected to the Shimada clan, the same powerful ninja clan that Hanzo and Genji were born into. Because of this relationship, Kiriko was trained alongside Shimada warriors, learning to master:

  • Stealth
  • Agility
  • Precision with kunai
  • Wall climbing and mobility techniques

The Fall of the Shimada Clan and Rise of the Hashimoto:

After the Shimada clan fractured, the criminal power vacuum in Kanezaka was filled by a new gang: the Hashimoto—a brutal yakuza group that began exploiting the people and asserting control through violence and fear.

When the Hashimoto began threatening the Kanezaka Shrine and the innocent people who lived nearby, Kiriko took matters into her own hands. She rejected the idea of standing by passively and became a masked vigilante, using her healing abilities, martial prowess, and spiritual powers to protect the city.

She became a symbol of hope, especially to those oppressed by the Hashimoto.

Conflict with Her Mother:

Kiriko’s mother disagreed with her daughter’s more dangerous path. She believed in healing from a place of peace, not violence. Kiriko, however, believed that in a world full of chaos and corruption, sometimes you have to fight to protect others.

Their relationship became strained—but Kiriko still carries both parts of her upbringing: the spiritual healer and the skilled fighter. She embraces both sides equally, refusing to choose.

Life as a Guardian and Hero:

Kiriko now operates independently as both a healer and protector. She watches over Kanezaka but also travels to other places in need. Unlike other heroes who align with major factions like Overwatch or Null Sector, Kiriko fights for the people, not for politics or power.

Though not officially a member of Overwatch, she aids them at times, especially as global conflict rises and the world faces new crises involving omnics, rogue artificial intelligence, and resistance movements.
