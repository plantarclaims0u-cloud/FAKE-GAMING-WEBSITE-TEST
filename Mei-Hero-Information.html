Character Summary

Mei (Mei-Ling Zhou) is a kind-hearted and optimistic climatologist in Overwatch 2.

She combines scientific brilliance with a gentle, hopeful personality, always striving to protect the environment and preserve life.

Despite her cheerful demeanor, Mei shows strong resilience and determination, especially in the face of isolation and loss.

She represents the theme of hope through science, using her knowledge and inventions to fight for a better future while supporting her allies with defensive and crowd-control abilities.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Mei was a leading scientist specializing in climate science and was recruited to join Overwatch as part of its environmental initiative.

She was stationed at a remote ecological observatory in Antarctica to study and combat worsening climate conditions. However, a catastrophic polar storm damaged the facility and cut the team off from the outside world.

With rescue impossible and supplies dwindling, Mei and her colleagues entered cryostasis in hopes of surviving until help arrived.

Mei was the only one who eventually awoke, discovering that many years had passed and her teammates had not survived.

Alone in the frozen wasteland, she repaired the station, adapted her technology for survival, and ultimately made her way back to civilization, carrying on her mission despite immense personal loss.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Served as a climatologist within Overwatch, focusing on environmental preservation.
  • Stationed at an Antarctic research facility studying climate threats.
  • Survived a catastrophic storm by entering cryostasis with her team.
  • Awoke years later as the sole survivor after system failures.
  • Adapted her technology (including her endothermic blaster) for survival and defense.
  • Successfully escaped Antarctica and rejoined the wider world.
  • Continues to support global efforts tied to Overwatch in protecting the planet.
