Brigitte

Basic Info

Full Name: Brigitte Lindholm

Role: Support (Survivor Class)

Age: 25

Brigitte Abilities

Rocket Flail

Brigitte's melee weapon has an extended range, enabling her to strike multiple enemies with a single swing.

Repair Pack

Brigitte throws a Repair Pack that can heal an ally. Any healing over that ally’s maximum health provides them with armor instead.

Whip Shot

Brigitte throws her flail a long distance, dealing damage and knocking an enemy away from her.

Barrier Shield

Brigitte deploys a frontal energy barrier to absorb a limited amount of damage.

Shield Bash

Once her Barrier Shield is deployed, Brigitte can dash forward to stun an enemy.

Rally (Ultimate)

Brigitte moves faster and provides all nearby allies with armor that lasts until it’s removed by damage.

Character Summary

Brigitte is a determined engineer-turned-warrior who blends compassion with courage, inspired by the ideals of classic heroes.

She fights to protect others while carrying forward the legacy of those who came before her.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Brigitte Lindholm grew up as the daughter of Torbjörn, learning engineering and mechanics while surrounded by Overwatch technology.

She later became the squire of Reinhardt, maintaining his armor during his travels.

Seeing the growing dangers of the world and wanting to do more than just support, she built her own armor and stepped into combat, becoming a hero dedicated to protecting others.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Served as squire and mechanic for Reinhardt during his travels.
  • Helped defend civilians from omnics and other threats.
  • Transitioned from support role into a frontline fighter.
  • Joined the reformed Overwatch after the recall.
