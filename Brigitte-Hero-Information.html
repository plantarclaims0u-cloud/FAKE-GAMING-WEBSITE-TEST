Brigitte

Basic Info

Full Name: Brigitte Lindholm

Role: Support (Survivor Class)

Age: 25

Brigitte Abilities

Rocket Flail

Brigitte's melee weapon has an extended range, enabling her to strike multiple enemies with a single swing.

Repair Pack

Brigitte throws a Repair Pack that can heal an ally. Any healing over that ally’s maximum health provides them with armor instead.

Whip Shot

Brigitte throws her flail a long distance, dealing damage and knocking an enemy away from her.

Barrier Shield

Brigitte deploys a frontal energy barrier to absorb a limited amount of damage.

Shield Bash

Once her Barrier Shield is deployed, Brigitte can dash forward to stun an enemy.

Rally (Ultimate)

Brigitte moves faster and provides all nearby allies with armor that lasts until it’s removed by damage.

Backstory

Juno Teo Minh was born on Mars—not metaphorically, but literally the first human child born on another planet. Her birth was part of a secretive project called Red Promise, an ambitious terraforming and colonization mission initiated by Lucheng Interstellar, the same organization responsible for Horizon Lunar Colony.

The goal of Red Promise was to create a sustainable human presence on Mars, with Juno as a symbol of that future—a generation of people born and raised beyond Earth. She was raised in a sterile, isolated base by scientists and AI, surrounded by machines, research data, and simulations designed to prepare her for life as a Martian pioneer. She learned about Earth through media, files, and documentaries, but never experienced it firsthand. Her childhood was full of questions—scientific ones about physics and biology, and personal ones about family, emotion, and meaning.

As she grew, so did the Martian colony. But success didn’t last.A catastrophic failure struck the project: devastating dust storms, atmospheric collapses, communication blackouts, and power loss. One by one, the scientists began disappearing—some evacuated, others lost. The terraforming systems failed, and Mars became once again a red tomb. Juno, still a teenager, was suddenly alone.

Before contact with Earth was completely lost, her caretakers entrusted her with a desperate mission: go to Earth and find help. Specifically, she was told to find Dr. Jiayi, a renowned climatologist and former colleague of Mei (Mei-Ling Zhou), who had once worked with Lucheng Interstellar. The plan: deliver the Red Promise data, warn Earth, and maybe—just maybe—save what was left of Mars’ dream.

Juno traveled from Mars to Earth in a stasis pod. When she awoke, everything was loud, overwhelming, and foreign. Earth’s gravity, its air, its chaos—it was all unlike anything she had known. She was completely out of place, and yet determined to complete her mission.She eventually found Mei, who took her in and helped her adapt to life on Earth. Mei became a mentor and guardian of sorts, teaching Juno not just science, but humanity—how to connect, how to feel, how to be more than a mission. Now, Juno fights with Overwatch, using her advanced Martian tech and her scientific mind to help others. Though still awkward and overly analytical, she’s fiercely empathetic and driven by a sense of hope—a belief that the future still matters, no matter what planet it’s on.
