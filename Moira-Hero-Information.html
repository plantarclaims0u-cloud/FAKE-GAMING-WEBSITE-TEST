Character Summary

Moira is a brilliant but ruthless scientist who values progress above morality, willing to cross ethical lines in pursuit of discovery.

Cold and calculating, she aligns herself with power to continue her research without limits.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Moira O’Deorain is an Irish geneticist from Dublin. A morally unbound genius, Moira prioritizes scientific advancement above ethics—keen to push humanity forward, regardless of cost.

She quickly gained notoriety for controversial research into directed genetic modification at the cellular level—a methodology even other scientists deemed ethically dubious .

After her controversial methods stalled her academic career, Moira joined Blackwatch—Overwatch’s covert ops branch—where she clandestinely continued genetic experiments, including on Gabriel Reyes (the future Reaper) .

As she grew, so did the Martian colony. But success didn’t last.A catastrophic failure struck the project: devastating dust storms, atmospheric collapses, communication blackouts, and power loss. One by one, the scientists began disappearing—some evacuated, others lost. The terraforming systems failed, and Mars became once again a red tomb. Juno, still a teenager, was suddenly alone.

Following Overwatch’s downfall, she aligned with Talon, continuing research and receiving backing from the Oasis scientific collective, where she held the position of Minister of Genetics.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Made major advancements in genetic science before being exiled for unethical experiments.

  • Played a key role in stabilizing and sustaining Reaper’s condition.
  • Contributes to Talon’s long-term goals through scientific experimentation.
