Character Summary

Reaper (real name: Gabriel Reyes) is a deadly, shadow-wreathed assassin and one of the most feared figures in the Overwatch universe.

Once a decorated soldier and leader, Reyes became disillusioned with Overwatch and ultimately transformed into a vengeful operative who now seeks to dismantle the organization he helped build.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Gabriel Reyes began as a highly respected soldier and was recruited into the original Overwatch strike team.

He fought during the Omnic Crisis and was known for his effectiveness and leadership resulting in him becoming one of Overwatch’s founding members.

Initially, Reyes was chosen to lead Overwatch before Jack Morrison was publicly appointed instead.

To maintain control over covert and morally ambiguous operations, Overwatch created Blackwatch, placing Reyes in command.

They Operated in secrecy and handled missions regular Overwatch could not as they recruited operatives like Genji Shimada.

Over time, reyes grew resentful of Overwatch leadership and began clashing with Morrison.

Tensions between Reyes and Morrison escalated into open conflict.

This culminated in a catastrophic event at Overwatch’s Swiss headquarters.

The two fought directly resulting in the facility being destroyed in an explosion and both were presumed dead.

This marked the official fall of Overwatch.

Reyes did not die—instead, he was transformed through experimental procedures linked to Moira O'Deorain.

His body became unstable at a molecular level and he exists in a constant state of decay and regeneration as he gained shadow-based abilities.

Because of this he suffers as hes in constant pain.

As a result of this, his humanity is deeply eroded.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • As leader of Blackwatch, reyes conducted covert global missions, eliminated high-value targets, and helped shape Overwatch’s darker side. These operations laid the groundwork for his eventual downfall.
  • The clash between Reyes and Jack Morrison resulted in massive destruction and exposed internal corruption which led to Overwatch being shut down.
  • After becoming Reaper, he joined Talon, a global terrorist organization. Within Talon, he is a high-ranking enforcer that carries out assassinations and destabilization missions while pursuing his own vendetta against former Overwatch agents.
  • Reaper systematically targets former Overwatch members and key global figures.
  • Reaper attacks a facility in Dorado with intent to steal valuable technology and he breaks in and confronts Winston. This event triggers Winston to issue the global recall, reforming Overwatch.
  • Reaper in present time is still a core operative within Talon and is actively opposing the reformation of Overwatch as he is driven by vengeance and ideological conflict.
