Character Summary

Genji Shimada is a cybernetic ninja and former assassin who struggles to reconcile his violent past with his search for inner peace.

Born into a powerful criminal family, Genji rejected his responsibilities—only to be nearly killed by his own brother.

Rebuilt as a cyborg by Overwatch, he became a weapon before eventually finding spiritual balance under the guidance of Zenyatta.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Born into the Shimada Clan, genji was the youngest son of the Shimada Clan, a powerful criminal syndicate based in Japan.

Genji lived a life of privilege and excess where he avoided responsibility within the clan and focused on pleasure rather than leadership.

His carefree attitude caused tension with his older brother, Hanzo Shimada, who took clan duties seriously.

After their father’s death, Hanzo was ordered to straighten Genji out.

When Genji refused hanzo confronted him and the confrontation escalated into violence and genji was seemingly killed.

Genji was secretly rescued by Angela Ziegler (Mercy) and brought to Overwatch.

His body was heavily damaged and overwatch rebuilt him using advanced cybernetics.

By then, he became a cybernetic operative.

However, he rejected his new body and struggled with identity and self-hatred and felt more weapon than human.

Then, when genji joined Overwatch’s covert division, Blackwatch, led by Gabriel Reyes.

Blackwatch was used for high-risk and morally ambiguous missions and genji became an elite assassin.

This is where genji continued to feel disconnected from humanity.

After Overwatch’s fall, Genji left and wandered the world.

He eventually met Zenyatta, an omnic monk who helped him accept his cybernetic form.

Zenyatta helped genji find inner peace and understand that he is more than his body.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Genji confronts Hanzo at their family home when Hanzo believes Genji is dead and they fight using their spirit dragons.
  • Genji reveals his identity and tells Hanzo: “You were only doing what you thought was right.”
  • As part of Blackwatch, Genji: Conducted covert operations worldwide, helped dismantle criminal organizations, and operated alongside dangerous figures like Gabriel Reyes. These missions deepened his internal struggle with violence.
  • After Overwatch collapsed: Genji traveled the world seeking meaning, avoided his past life and responsibilities, and focused on self-discovery and balance.
  • When Winston issues the recall: Genji answers the call, rejoins the fight to protect the world, and now acts with clarity and purpose.
