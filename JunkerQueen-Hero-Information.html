Junker Queen's Abilities

Scattergun

A pump-action shotgun that hits hardest up close but still has some mid-range poke. Best used while weaving in and out of fights.

Commanding Shout

Gives Junker Queen bonus health and a speed boost, plus a smaller boost to nearby teammates. Great for engaging, escaping, or sustaining during a fight.

Carnage

A wide axe swing that hits multiple enemies in front of her and applies a strong bleed. Big damage if you land it on several targets.

Jagged Blade

Throws a knife that sticks into an enemy and applies a bleed. You can reactivate to pull the enemy toward you, setting up close-range damage. Also works as a quick melee bleed if used up close.

Rampage (Ultimate)

She charges forward, dealing damage, applying wounds, and inflicting anti-heal (enemies can’t be healed for a short time). It’s powerful for initiating fights and shutting down enemy supports.

Character Summary

Junker Queen (real name: Odessa “Dez” Stone) is the ruthless and charismatic ruler of Junkertown, a harsh, lawless settlement in the Australian Outback.

She’s a frontline tank hero known for aggressive, close-range combat and a “wounds-based” self-healing mechanic.

She thrives on chaos and strength, embodying the “rule by power” philosophy of Junkertown.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:
