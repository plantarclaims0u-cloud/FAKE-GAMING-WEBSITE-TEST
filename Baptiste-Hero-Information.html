Baptiste

Basic Info

Full Name: Jean‑Baptiste Augustin

Age: 38

Role: Support/Healer

Baptiste Abilities

Biotic Launcher

Primary Fire: A three-round burst hitscan weapon with headshots that deals solid damage and rewards accuracy. Useful for pressuring enemies. Secondary Fire: A lobbed healing projectile that heals allies within a ~3 m radius on impact. Vital for mid-fight area healing.

Regenerative Burst

Heals Baptiste instantly and over time for nearby allies. Cooldown ~14 s. Critical for clutch healing.

Immortality Field

Throws a device that creates a field preventing any ally inside from dropping below ~20% HP. It interrupts kills, including environmental death. Lasts ~5 s; device has ~150 HP and can be destroyed. Ideal for negating ultimates like D.Va’s Self-Destruct. Around 6.5 m radius.

Exo Boots

Holding crouch charges a super-jump. No cooldown. Great for high-ground positioning and unusual vantage points.

Amplification Matrix (Ultimate)

Deploys a rectangular matrix field that doubles both damage and healing of friendly projectiles passing through it. Meant to empower DPS and allied supports. Orientation can be rotated before deployment. Does not amplify melee swings. Extremely powerful when used with hitscan or projectile heroes.

Backstory

Baptiste is a Haitian combat medic, one of 30 million children orphaned by the Omnic Crisis. He grew up in chaos and hardship after the war devastated his homeland, choosing survival at any cost.

He enlisted in the Caribbean Coalition’s special ops, serving as a combat medic and honing his skills in battlefield medicine and improvisation.

After his discharge, he joined Talon, initially for steady work and to fund a clinic back home. Over time, he realized Talon’s missions included unjust assassinations and civilian casualties, meaning he was complicit in violence—something he couldn’t stand.

He defected from Talon, becoming a fugitive. Talon targeted him relentlessly; however, those who pursued him were never seen again. Baptiste continued traveling the world providing aid to the wounded and oppressed.

His journey intersects with former Talon ally Mauga, who provided critical intel on agents being hunted. Baptiste sought out Dr. Angela Ziegler (Mercy) to warn her and help reform Overwatch. He later connected with Cassidy, who recruited him to the reformed Overwatch team.

Baptiste is described as someone who acknowledges his past errors—but strives to make up for them now.
