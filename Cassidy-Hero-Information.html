Character Summary

Cole Cassidy is a rugged, gunslinging outlaw-turned-hero known for his sharp aim, calm demeanor, and strong personal code of justice.

Once a criminal drifting through life, Cassidy eventually sought redemption by joining Overwatch’s black-ops division, becoming a skilled operative.

In Overwatch 2, he operates as a freelance gunslinger and recruiter, helping rebuild Overwatch while choosing jobs that align with his morals.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Cassidy was born in Santa Fe and raised on a farm in Texas. From a young age, he learned how to shoot and survive, but he quickly developed a rebellious streak.

He got into trouble early (e.g., theft and arrests), drifted between jobs and locations, and eventually adopted the alias “Jesse McCree” to escape his past.

Cassidy became a founding member of the Deadlock Gang, a group involved in weapons trafficking and organized crime across the American Southwest.

Cassidy’s life changed when Overwatch agents captured the Deadlock Gang in a sting operation.

He was given a choice: Rot in prison or join Blackwatch.

He chose Blackwatch, seeing it as a chance to: Escape his past, put his skills to better use, and to operate with fewer restrictions than standard Overwatch agents.

Within Blackwatch, Cassidy became: A highly effective operative and known for handling dangerous, morally gray missions.

Despite this, he began developing a sense of responsibility and justice, moving away from his criminal mindset.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • As a member of Blackwatch, Cassidy: Took part in covert missions worldwide, worked alongside figures like Gabriel Reyes (Reaper), and thrived in high-risk, off-the-books operations These missions shaped his skills—and his internal conflict about morality.
  • When Overwatch began to collapse: Internal conflicts (especially within Blackwatch) escalated, and cassidy chose to leave and go underground. He abandoned his past identity and began drifting again—this time as a bounty hunter and mercenary.
  • When Winston sends out a global recall to reform Overwatch: Cassidy initially refuses to rejoin and sends Echo in his place. But later, persuaded by Ana Amari, he Joins the effort and takes on a leadership role in rebuilding the team.
