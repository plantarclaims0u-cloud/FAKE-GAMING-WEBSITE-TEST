Character Summary

Echo is an advanced, adaptive artificial intelligence in Overwatch 2, designed to learn and evolve rapidly from her environment.

Unlike most omnics, Echo represents a hopeful vision of human–AI coexistence, combining cutting-edge technology with emotional awareness and curiosity.

She is highly analytical but also empathetic, reflecting the values of her creator.

Echo’s defining trait is her ability to duplicate and adapt, both in combat and personality, making her one of the most versatile and unique beings in the Overwatch universe.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Echo was created by Dr. Mina Liao, a leading scientist and one of the founding members of Overwatch.

Dr. Liao designed Echo as a next-generation AI capable of learning from human behavior, with the goal of fostering peace between humans and omnics.

However, following Liao’s death during the Omnic Crisis, Echo was placed in quarantine by the government due to fears about her potential power and autonomy.

She remained inactive for years until she was eventually recovered and reactivated by Cole Cassidy, allowing her to finally fulfill her intended purpose.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Created as a highly advanced adaptive AI by Dr. Mina Liao.
  • Embodies a bridge between humans and omnics, reflecting Liao’s vision for peaceful coexistence.
  • Placed in quarantine and hidden away after concerns about her capabilities following the Omnic Crisis.
  • Recovered and reactivated by Cole Cassidy.
  • Joins the reformed Overwatch, contributing to its renewed mission.
  • Demonstrates her signature duplication ability, allowing her to replicate other heroes’ abilities in combat.
  • Represents one of the most advanced examples of AI development in the Overwatch universe.
