Character Summary

Pharah (Fareeha Amari) is a disciplined and honor-driven soldier in Overwatch 2, known for her strong sense of justice and duty.

She is highly principled, believing in order, protection, and doing what is right, often striving to live up to an ideal of heroism.

Pharah carries herself with confidence and professionalism, but beneath that is a deep desire to prove herself and step out of the shadow of her mother.

She represents the theme of legacy and responsibility, balancing personal ambition with a commitment to protecting others.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Pharah is the daughter of Ana Amari, one of the founding members of Overwatch.

Growing up, she admired Overwatch and dreamed of joining its ranks, inspired by her mother’s legacy as a hero.

However, Ana discouraged her from following that path, wanting to protect her from the dangers of that life.

Determined to forge her own identity, Fareeha enlisted in the military and eventually joined a private security force, where she gained recognition for her skill and leadership.

Equipped with the advanced Raptora combat suit, she became known as Pharah, dedicating herself to defending the innocent and upholding justice on a global scale.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Served in the military and later joined a private security organization.
  • Became a key operative equipped with the advanced Raptora combat suit.
  • Built a reputation as a highly skilled and dependable soldier.
  • Inspired by—but also struggled with—the legacy of Ana Amari.
  • Protected valuable assets and civilians in high-risk conflict zones.
  • Eventually answers the recall of Overwatch.
  • Plays an active role in global conflicts, fighting to maintain order and justice.
