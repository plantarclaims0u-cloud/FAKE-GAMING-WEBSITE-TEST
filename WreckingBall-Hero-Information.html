Character Summary

Wrecking Ball, also known as Hammond, is a highly intelligent hamster piloting a powerful mechanized combat suit.

He is resourceful, independent, and surprisingly expressive despite his small size.

His personality blends cunning survival instincts with a mischievous and daring attitude.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Hammond was a test subject on the Horizon Lunar Colony, where he was genetically enhanced like other animals.

Inspired by :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}, he engineered his own escape pod during the colony’s rebellion.

His pod crash-landed in the Australian Outback, where he modified it into a spherical combat mech.

He eventually made his way to Junkertown, competing in its gladiatorial arenas.

Through skill and ingenuity, he gained freedom and became a wandering adventurer.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Escaped the Horizon Lunar Colony using a self-built craft.
  • Engineered the Wrecking Ball mech from salvaged technology.
  • Survived and thrived in the harsh Junkertown environment.
  • Competed in and won Junkertown arena battles.
  • Represents intelligence and adaptability in unexpected forms.
