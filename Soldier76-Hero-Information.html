Character Summary

Soldier: 76 (real name: Jack Morrison) is a former Overwatch commander turned vigilante, dedicated to uncovering the truth behind the organization’s fall and bringing justice to those responsible.

nce the public face of Overwatch, Morrison now operates alone, targeting corruption and conspiracy while still holding onto the ideals that Overwatch was meant to represent.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Jack Morrison served as a soldier during the Omnic Crisis, where he demonstrated: Exceptional leadership, Tactical brilliance, and Strong moral conviction.

He was part of the Soldier Enhancement Program, which granted him enhanced physical abilities—strengthening him beyond a normal human.

Following the war, Morrison became a founding member of Overwatch alongside Gabriel Reyes.

Initially, Reyes was expected to lead until Morrison was ultimately chosen as the public commander.

As Overwatch’s leader, Morrison oversaw global peacekeeping operations, built Overwatch into a respected international force, and became a heroic public figure.

However, Internal corruption began to grow and conflicts within the organization intensified.

At Overwatch’s Swiss headquarters:

Morrison and Reyes engaged in a final battle and a massive explosion destroyed the facility that people assumed killed the two.

This event led to Overwatch being disbanded globally.

Morrison survived—but the world believed he was dead.

He re-emerged as Soldier: 76, a masked vigilante that operated outside the law and targets those responsible for Overwatch’s fall.

He also actively seeks the truth behind conspiracies involving Talon and former agents.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • As commander, Morrison: Led global missions during Overwatch’s golden age to help maintain international peace and Inspired trust and hope worldwide.
  • When the battle with Gabriel Reyes ensued it: Destroyed Overwatch headquarters as it exposed internal fractures and ended the organization entirely.
  • As Soldier: 76, he investigates corruption tied to Overwatch’s fall and attacks criminal and terrorist networks and as well targets talon operations.
  • During an attack by Reaper Soldier: 76 intervenes and protects civilians and counters Talon’s actions.
  • When Winston issues the Overwatch recall Morrison does not immediately rejoin but, chooses to continue working alone.
  • In present time, Soldier: 76 continues to remain a lone operative as he is focused on exposing truth and fighting Talon apart from Overwatch team.
