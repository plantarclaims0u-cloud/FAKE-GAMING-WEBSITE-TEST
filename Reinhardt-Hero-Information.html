Character Summary

Reinhardt Wilhelm is a larger-than-life German warrior who models himself after the knights of old.

Clad in massive Crusader armor and wielding a rocket-powered hammer, he fights not just to win, but to embody ideals of honor, courage, and protection.

Early in his life, he chased glory and recognition, but over time he transformed into a hero defined by responsibility and sacrifice.

He is loud, theatrical, and often humorous, but beneath that bravado is a deeply principled person who believes that strength means shielding others from harm.

Reinhardt isn’t just a soldier—he sees himself as a guardian, carrying forward a code of chivalry in a modern, war-torn world.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Reinhardt grew up in Germany, inspired by heroic legends and the romantic ideals of knighthood.

He eventually joined the Crusaders, an elite military unit formed to combat omnics during the Omnic Crisis.

These soldiers wore advanced powered armor, turning them into towering figures on the battlefield.

As a young Crusader, Reinhardt was impulsive and glory-driven.

He often disobeyed orders in his eagerness to prove himself, believing that heroism meant bold, visible acts of bravery.

His commanding officer, Balderich von Adler, tried to instill discipline and a deeper sense of duty in him, but Reinhardt struggled to fully understand those lessons.

That changed during a decisive battle.

Reinhardt’s recklessness contributed to a dangerous situation, and Balderich ultimately sacrificed himself to ensure the mission succeeded and Reinhardt survived.

This moment became the defining turning point of Reinhardt’s life.

He inherited his commander’s armor and, more importantly, his philosophy: that true honor lies not in personal glory, but in protecting others and fighting for something greater than oneself.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Reinhardt became one of the founding and most recognizable members of Overwatch, serving as a frontline tank and symbol of strength during global conflicts.
  • He formed a lifelong bond with Torbjörn, whose engineering expertise supported Reinhardt in battle and helped maintain his Crusader armor.
  • He took on a mentorship role for Brigitte, honoring a promise to protect her and eventually training alongside her as she became his squire and combat partner.

Post-Overwatch Activities:

After Overwatch was disbanded, Reinhardt refused to step away from heroism, instead traveling the world as an independent protector, continuing to fight injustice wherever he found it.

Return of Overwatch (Overwatch 2 Era):

When Winston initiated the recall to reform Overwatch, Reinhardt answered immediately, reaffirming his lifelong commitment to defending others.
