Character Summary

Winston is a highly intelligent, genetically engineered gorilla who combines scientific curiosity with strong moral conviction.

He is compassionate and idealistic, believing deeply in heroism and the mission of Overwatch.

Despite his immense strength and rage-fueled combat abilities, he prefers diplomacy and protecting others over violence.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Winston was one of several gorillas genetically enhanced on the Horizon Lunar Colony as part of a scientific experiment.

Unlike the others, he was mentored by a human scientist named Dr. Harold Winston, who inspired his love of science and humanity.

After a rebellion led by another gorilla, Winston escaped to Earth using a homemade rocket.

He later joined Overwatch, adopting the name of his mentor and dedicating himself to its cause.

Following Overwatch’s fall, he became one of the key figures trying to reform the organization.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Played a central role as a scientist and hero within Overwatch.
  • Issued the recall that attempted to reunite Overwatch agents.
  • Represents hope, intelligence, and idealism in the Overwatch world.
  • Fought against threats like Talon and Null Sector.
  • Continues working to restore Overwatch’s mission.
