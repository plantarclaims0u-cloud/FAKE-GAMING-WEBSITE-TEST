Roadhog

Basic Info

Real Name: Mako Rutledge

Role: Tank (Bruiser Class)

Age: 50

Roadhog's Abilities

Scrap Gun

Short-range blast that deals huge damage up close—devastating after a hook. Secondary fire fires a ball that travels forward and explodes into a shotgun-like burst at mid-range, giving him some poke.

Chain Hook

Throws a hook that pulls an enemy directly to him, setting up an easy close-range combo. This is his signature pick tool.

Take a Breather

Heals a large portion of his health and reduces incoming damage while active. Keeps him alive without relying much on supports.

Whole Hog (Ultimate)

Unleashes a rapid-fire barrage that knocks enemies back and deals heavy sustained damage. Great for zoning, environmental kills, or shredding tanks at close range.

Character Summary

Roadhog is a ruthless and enigmatic enforcer known for his brutality and silence.

He rarely speaks, expressing himself through actions rather than words.

His imposing presence and unpredictable nature make him both feared and difficult to understand.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Mako Rutledge lived in Australia before the Omnium explosion turned the Outback into a wasteland.

The disaster transformed both the land and its people, pushing him into a harsh survivalist lifestyle.

He eventually became a mercenary and bodyguard, partnering with Junkrat.

Over time, he adopted the persona of Roadhog, becoming more ruthless and detached.

His past identity faded as he embraced life in chaos.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Survived the destruction of the Australian Outback.
  • Formed a partnership with Junkrat.
  • Became a feared enforcer and criminal.
  • Operates as a bodyguard and mercenary.
  • Represents the harsh consequences of the Omnium disaster.
