Character Summary

Bastion (designation: SST Laboratories Siege Automaton E54) is a former war machine turned peaceful wanderer.

Originally built as a combat robot, it now roams the wilderness with a childlike curiosity about nature.

Despite its gentle personality, Bastion still contains dormant combat programming that can activate under threat—transforming it into a highly destructive weapon.

This duality (peaceful observer vs. unstoppable weapon) defines its character.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Bastion units were mass-produced robots designed for peacekeeping and combat support. They had a unique ability to transform into powerful turret weapons.

During the Omnic Crisis, Bastion units were reprogrammed (or overtaken) and turned against humanity, becoming the backbone of the omnic army.

They became symbols of destruction and fear due to their overwhelming numbers and firepower.

Near the end of the war, one Bastion unit (the one we know) was severely damaged in battle near Eichenwalde, Germany, and left inactive in the wilderness.

For over a decade: It remained dormant, Nature grew over it (vines, moss, animals nesting), It was essentially “forgotten”.

Bastion reactivated unexpectedly when Ganymede disturbed it while building a nest.

But something had changed: Its combat programming was partially lost, It developed curiosity instead of aggression, It began exploring the world rather than fighting it.
