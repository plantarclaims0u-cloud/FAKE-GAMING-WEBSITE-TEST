Character Summary

Juno is a highly intelligent and curious support hero with a strong focus on advanced technology and exploration.

Coming from a background tied to space and scientific research, she brings a unique perspective to the battlefield, using cutting-edge tools to aid her team.

Though new to Earth, she is determined to help others and prove herself, driven by optimism, curiosity, and a desire to make a positive impact.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Juno Teo Minh was born on Mars—not metaphorically, but literally the first human child born on another planet. Her birth was part of a secretive project called Red Promise, an ambitious terraforming and colonization mission initiated by Lucheng Interstellar, the same organization responsible for Horizon Lunar Colony.

The goal of Red Promise was to create a sustainable human presence on Mars, with Juno as a symbol of that future—a generation of people born and raised beyond Earth. She was raised in a sterile, isolated base by scientists and AI, surrounded by machines, research data, and simulations designed to prepare her for life as a Martian pioneer. She learned about Earth through media, files, and documentaries, but never experienced it firsthand. Her childhood was full of questions—scientific ones about physics and biology, and personal ones about family, emotion, and meaning.

As she grew, so did the Martian colony. But success didn’t last.A catastrophic failure struck the project: devastating dust storms, atmospheric collapses, communication blackouts, and power loss. One by one, the scientists began disappearing—some evacuated, others lost. The terraforming systems failed, and Mars became once again a red tomb. Juno, still a teenager, was suddenly alone.

Before contact with Earth was completely lost, her caretakers entrusted her with a desperate mission: go to Earth and find help. Specifically, she was told to find Dr. Jiayi, a renowned climatologist and former colleague of Mei (Mei-Ling Zhou), who had once worked with Lucheng Interstellar. The plan: deliver the Red Promise data, warn Earth, and maybe—just maybe—save what was left of Mars’ dream.

Juno traveled from Mars to Earth in a stasis pod. When she awoke, everything was loud, overwhelming, and foreign. Earth’s gravity, its air, its chaos—it was all unlike anything she had known. She was completely out of place, and yet determined to complete her mission.She eventually found Mei, who took her in and helped her adapt to life on Earth.

Mei became a mentor and guardian of sorts, teaching Juno not just science, but humanity—how to connect, how to feel, how to be more than a mission.Now, Juno fights with Overwatch, using her advanced Martian tech and her scientific mind to help others. Though still awkward and overly analytical, she’s fiercely empathetic and driven by a sense of hope—a belief that the future still matters, no matter what planet it’s on.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Traveled from a space-based colony to Earth, marking her first direct involvement with global conflicts.
  • Introduced advanced space-age technology to aid allies in combat and support roles.
  • Joined the new generation of heroes answering the Overwatch recall.
  • Works to adapt to Earth while assisting in missions against global threats.
  • Represents the expansion of humanity beyond Earth and brings a hopeful, forward-looking perspective to the team.
