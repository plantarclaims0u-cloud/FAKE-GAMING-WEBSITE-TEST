Character Summary

Orisa is a protective and optimistic omnic designed to safeguard others.

She is polite and thoughtful, always prioritizing the safety of civilians.

Her personality reflects both innocence and a strong sense of duty.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Orisa was built by a young engineering prodigy named Efi Oladele in Numbani.

She was constructed using parts from OR15 defense robots that had previously failed to stop threats.

Efi reprogrammed and improved her, giving her adaptive intelligence and a protective mindset.

Orisa quickly became a guardian of Numbani, learning and growing with each encounter.

She continues evolving as she gains experience in protecting others.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Defends the city of Numbani from threats.
  • Represents a new generation of adaptive omnics.
  • Learns and evolves through real-world experience.
  • Acts as a symbol of hope and protection.
  • Demonstrates the positive potential of AI.
