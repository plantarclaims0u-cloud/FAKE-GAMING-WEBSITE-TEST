Character Summary

Ashe (Elizabeth Caledonia “Calamity” Ashe) is a calculating and ambitious outlaw leader in Overwatch 2.

As the head of the Deadlock Gang, she stands out for bringing structure and discipline to crime, preferring strategy and control over chaos.

Ashe is fiercely independent, shaped by a deep need to assert control over her life after a neglected upbringing.

While outwardly composed and authoritative, her leadership style reflects a desire to build the order and loyalty she never had growing up, with her omnic companion B.O.B. acting as both enforcer and the closest thing she has to family.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Ashe was born into a wealthy and powerful family connected to the Arbalest Arms, but her privileged upbringing was emotionally hollow.

Her parents were largely absent, leaving her to be raised by their omnic butler, B.O.B., who became her primary source of stability.

Despite access to elite education and opportunity, Ashe grew resentful of the empty, controlled life laid out for her.

As a teenager, she met Cole Cassidy, and through their shared acts of rebellion and crime, she discovered both excitement and a sense of purpose.

Rejecting her family’s expectations entirely, Ashe chose to carve her own path as an outlaw, using crime not just for survival, but as a way to define her identity and independence.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Co-founded and later led the Deadlock Gang, transforming it into a powerful and organized criminal group.
  • Unified multiple gangs across the American Southwest, creating a structured criminal network.
  • Developed a personal “code” among outlaws, reducing infighting and increasing efficiency.
  • Experienced a major falling-out with Cole Cassidy after he left the gang.
  • Participated in the train heist depicted in the Reunion cinematic, where she clashes with Cassidy.
  • Expanded her influence beyond regional crime, increasing her notoriety as a major outlaw figure.
  • Formed an alliance with Talon, tying her operations into global conflicts.
