Ashe Abilities

The Viper

A rifle that can hip-fire quickly or scope in for slower, higher-damage shots.

Dynamite

Throws an explosive that detonates after a delay or when shot, dealing area damage + burn over time.

A short-range blast that knocks enemies back and launches Ashe away, mainly used for mobility/escape.

B.O.B. (Ultimate)

Summons B.O.B. who charges forward, knocks enemies up, then auto-shoots like a teammate.
