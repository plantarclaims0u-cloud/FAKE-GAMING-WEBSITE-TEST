Character Summary

Junkrat (real name: Jamison Fawkes) is an explosive-obsessed anarchist, scavenger, and treasure hunter known for his chaotic personality and love of destruction.

Unpredictable and manic, Junkrat thrives on chaos—but beneath his wild behavior lies a surprisingly clever and resourceful survivor of a harsh, irradiated world.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Junkrat was born in the harsh wastelands of the Australian Outback, a region transformed by war and radiation following the Omnic Crisis.

He grew up in a lawless, irradiated environment where survival depended on scavenging and improvisation.

This is where he developed a fascination with explosives early on.

After the Omnic Crisis, Australia attempted to deal with omnic infrastructure by destroying a massive omnium.

The explosion devastated the region and created a radioactive wasteland where survivors formed a settlement known as Junkertown.

Junkrat lived among the scavengers and criminals of Junkertown until he discovered a valuable secret (implied to be a hidden treasure within the omnium ruins).

His erratic behavior and obsession made him dangerous.

He was eventually banished by the ruling authorities, including Junker Queen.

After exile, Junkrat formed a partnership with Roadhog (Mako Rutledge).

Junkrat provides brains and explosives while Roadhog provides muscle and intimidation.

Despite their differences, they become inseparable partners in crime and travel the world as outlaws and mercenaries.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Junkrat’s most defining trait is his obsession with a mysterious treasure. He claims to know the location of immense riches and this knowledge makes him a target that leads to constant pursuits and conflicts.
  • With Roadhog, Junkrat commits robberies and sabotage across the world causing large-scale destruction. He becomes internationally wanted and their exploits are chaotic rather than strategic—often driven by impulse and greed.
  • Junkrat and Roadhog attempt a heist in Junkertown.
  • Despite his reckless nature, Junkrat somehow survives extremely dangerous situations and adapts quickly in combat.
  • Junkrat continues traveling the world causing chaos while chasing wealth and explosions as he workes with Roadhog as a criminal duo.
