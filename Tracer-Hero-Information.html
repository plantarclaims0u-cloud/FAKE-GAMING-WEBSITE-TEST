Character Summary

Tracer (Lena Oxton) is an energetic, optimistic, and time-jumping adventurer in Overwatch 2. Known for her upbeat personality and quick wit, she serves as one of the most recognizable faces of Overwatch.

Tracer is driven by a strong moral compass and a desire to help others, often acting as a symbol of hope even in difficult times.

Despite the instability of her condition, she embraces life with enthusiasm and courage, representing the theme of resilience and positivity in the face of uncertainty.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Tracer was a pilot selected for an experimental teleportation aircraft developed by Overwatch.

During its maiden flight, the aircraft malfunctioned and disappeared, leaving Lena presumed dead.

When she reappeared months later, she suffered from “chronal disassociation,” causing her to randomly slip through time and making it impossible for her to maintain a stable physical presence.

Her condition was eventually stabilized by a device known as the chronal accelerator, allowing her to control her time shifts.

With her new abilities, Tracer became a key Overwatch agent, using her speed and time-manipulation powers to fight for good and protect others.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Selected as a test pilot for an experimental teleportation program within Overwatch.
  • Survived a catastrophic accident that caused chronal disassociation.
  • Stabilized by the chronal accelerator, allowing control over her time-jumping abilities.
  • Became one of the most prominent and recognizable agents of Overwatch.
  • Played a key role in numerous missions as a fast-response operative.
  • Remained committed to Overwatch’s ideals even after its disbandment.
  • Answers the recall in Overwatch 2, continuing to fight for global peace.
