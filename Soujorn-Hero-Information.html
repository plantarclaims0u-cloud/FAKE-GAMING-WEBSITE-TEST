Character Summary

Sojourn (Vivian Chase) is a highly disciplined and mission-focused cybernetic soldier in Overwatch 2.

Known for her calm leadership and tactical precision, she embodies professionalism and accountability, often placing the mission and her team above all else.

Sojourn is pragmatic and decisive, shaped by years of military service and augmented with advanced cybernetics that enhance her combat effectiveness.

She represents the theme of duty and sacrifice, balancing her humanity with the increasing presence of technology in her body and life.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Sojourn was born with a chronic illness that required extensive medical intervention, eventually leading to the gradual replacement of much of her body with cybernetic enhancements.

Rather than holding her back, this experience shaped her resilience and adaptability.

She joined the military at a young age, where her natural leadership and tactical skill quickly set her apart.

During the Omnic Crisis, she served with distinction and was later recruited into Overwatch.

Over time, she rose through the ranks to become a respected commander, playing a key role in coordinating missions and guiding the organization’s operations.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Served with distinction during the Omnic Crisis.
  • Recruited into Overwatch and rose to a leadership position.
  • Acted as a key commander, coordinating missions and strategic operations.
  • Became one of the most trusted and reliable leaders within Overwatch.
  • Witnessed and was affected by the organization’s eventual disbandment.
  • Answers the recall in Overwatch 2, helping to reform Overwatch.
  • Uses advanced cybernetic enhancements to enhance mobility, strength, and combat precision.
