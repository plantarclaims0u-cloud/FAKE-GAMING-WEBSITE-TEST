Basic Info

Name: Ana Amari

Role: Support (Tactician Class)

Age: 62

Ana Abilities

Biotic Rifle

Fires darts that heal allies and damage enemies. Unscoped shots are projectile-based; scoped fire delivers hitscan shots through barriers.

Sleep Dart

A non-lethal dart that puts a single enemy to sleep for a few seconds. Sleeping enemies wake upon taking damage.

Biotic Grenade

On impact, heals allies and gives them a boost to healing received. Damages enemies and prevents them from being healed for a short period.

Nano Boost (Ultimate)

Enhances a teammate with Increased damage output, Reduced damage received, A burst of instant healing on cast.

Character Summary

Ana is a veteran sniper and one of Overwatch’s original founders, known for her discipline, leadership, and strong sense of duty.

She acts as a mentor figure to younger heroes, guided by experience and a desire to protect others from the mistakes of the past.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Ana Amari is an Egyptian sniper, one of the founding members of Overwatch and mother to Pharah (Fareeha Amari).

During the Omnic Crisis, she became known as one of the best snipers in the world, then served as Jack Morrison’s (Soldier: 76) second-in-command.

In a fateful mission, Ana was believed to be killed by Widowmaker (Amélie Lacroix) after hesitating to shoot her, which resulted in losing her right eye and retreating from Overwatch.

Years later, she returned to action in Overwatch 2—guided by Winston’s Recall—to protect her homeland and reforge the organization, though she encouraged a new generation of heroes to lead instead.

Personality-wise, Ana is a strong-willed, responsible leader—often personally taking on the duty of protecting her team. She deeply cares for them and strongly supports her daughter’s path.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Founding member of Overwatch and key leader during the Omnic Crisis.
  • Presumed dead after a mission involving Widowmaker, where she was severely injured.
  • Spent years operating in secret, protecting people from the shadows.
  • Returned to action after Winston’s recall to help rebuild Overwatch.
