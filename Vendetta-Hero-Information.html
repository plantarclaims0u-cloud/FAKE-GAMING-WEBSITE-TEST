Vendetta Abilities

Palatine Fang

Hard-light greatsword with combo swings ending in a destructive overhead slash.

Warding Stance

Block with your sword to reduce frontal damage and deflect melee attacks. Blocking damage depletes energy.

Projected Edge

Available while in Warding Stance; consume energy to fire a wide projectile.

Whirlwind Dash

Rush forward into a circular slash, repositioning or disengaging quickly.

Soaring Slice

Hurl your sword in the direction you’re facing, then fly to it; can follow up with a downward slash.

Sundering Blade (Ultimate)

A powerful cleave that cuts through most defenses, dealing severe damage to enemies in front of you.

Character Summary

Vendetta, the She-Wolf of Retaliation (Italian: "La Lupa Della Ritorsione") is a gladiator at Colosseo.

Having carved out her place among the arena’s elite, she cuts down enemies with ruthless, regal precision. Beneath the surface, her ambitions run deeper.

Each gladiatorial victory is a calculated step towards something greater.

Amid the roars of the Colosseo crowd, she steps into the spotlight...and she's not going anywhere without a fight.

Vendetta enjoys pushing limits,and her quest for power is transparrent.

She makes no effort to hide her true nature.

She wields her anger like a blade, and will destroy anything that stands in her way.

For her, everything is about spectacle; a combination of actor and fighter, possessed of complete confidence.

She has known loss and injustice. Has been both respected and disrespected.

She wants power, but remembers what it is to be without it.

She knows what it is to be hungry and hurt, and she will never allow herself to forget.

As both a member of high society and a gladiator, she has learnt to balance both worlds.

Experience has taught her to not rush in, just swinging a sword, and to always play both sides to her advantage.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Marzia Bartalotti was born into a powerful and dangerous family, the daughter of Antonio Bartalotti, a Talon-affiliated arms dealer.

Her childhood was shaped by both luxury and instability, especially after losing her mother at a young age.

Growing up surrounded by influence and violence, she became determined to define herself on her own terms rather than simply inherit status.

Rejecting the safety of elite society, she entered the Colosseo arena to earn respect through combat.

There, she crafted the identity of “Vendetta,” a name that reflects her internal drive for revenge and her need to reclaim control over her life and destiny.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Rose to prominence as a top gladiator in the Colosseo arena.
  • Developed a signature fighting style based on endurance and retaliation.
  • Became both a public celebrity and a feared combatant.
  • Built influence among elite circles while maintaining her arena persona
  • Maintains connections to Talon-linked networks.
