Ramattra Abilities

Void Accelerator

Fires a continuous stream of nanite projectiles from his staff, dealing consistent damage to enemies at range.

Nemesis Form

Punches enemies with powerful, barrier-piercing attacks in close range.

Void Barrier

Deploys a movable energy barrier that blocks incoming attacks, protecting himself and allies.

Creates a field of nanites that slows enemies and pulls them toward its center, dealing damage over time.

Annihilation (Ultimate)

Automatically shifts Ramattra into Nemesis Form and deals heavy area damage to all nearby enemies.

Character Summary

Ramattra is the founder of Null Sector, a militant omnic organization advocating for the rights and liberation of omnics.

Ramattra is a strategic, authoritarian figure who seeks to reshape the world to ensure omnic survival and dominance, often by force.

He can shift between Omnic Form (ranged, tactical control) and Nemesis Form (melee, heavily armored combat), making him a versatile frontline presence on the battlefield.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Ramattra was created as an omnic during the rise of omnics in Shambali Monastery, where he was mentored in omnic philosophy and ethics.

Unlike many omnics, he developed a deep awareness of inequality and oppression, observing how omnics were treated as second-class entities by humans.

Through his studies and experiences, Ramattra became a strong proponent of omnic self-determination.

Witnessing continued human aggression and discrimination against omnics fueled his determination to take decisive action for omnic survival.

He ultimately broke away from the peaceful philosophy of his mentors to form a more militant organization.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Founder of Null Sector: Ramattra established Null Sector as a militant omnic group dedicated to achieving equality for omnics, even if it means using force.
  • Omnic Uprisings: He led multiple organized attacks in major cities, challenging human authorities and asserting omnic presence on the global stage.
  • Battlefield Innovations: Ramattra’s ability to switch between Omnic Form and Nemesis Form has set a precedent for hybrid combat tactics among omnic forces.
  • Strategic Leadership: He is known for coordinating large-scale operations that blend offensive assaults with defensive control, emphasizing both territory control and direct engagement.
  • Philosophical Influence: While militant, he is deeply philosophical, using his speeches and actions to inspire other omnics to rise and challenge oppression.
