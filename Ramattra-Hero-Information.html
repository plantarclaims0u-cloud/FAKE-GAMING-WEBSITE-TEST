Name: Ramattra

Ramattra Abilities

Void Accelerator

Fires a continuous stream of nanite projectiles from his staff, dealing consistent damage to enemies at range.

Nemesis Form

Punches enemies with powerful, barrier-piercing attacks in close range.

Void Barrier

Deploys a movable energy barrier that blocks incoming attacks, protecting himself and allies.

Ravenous Vortex

Creates a field of nanites that slows enemies and pulls them toward its center, dealing damage over time.

Annihilation (Ultimate)

Automatically shifts Ramattra into Nemesis Form and deals heavy area damage to all nearby enemies.
