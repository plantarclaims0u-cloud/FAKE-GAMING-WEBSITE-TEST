Character Summary

Hanzo Shimada is a master archer, former heir to a powerful criminal empire, and a man burdened by guilt and honor.

Once destined to lead the Shimada Clan, Hanzo was forced into a life of violence and discipline. After a tragic confrontation with his brother Genji Shimada, he abandoned his title and now wanders the world seeking redemption.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Heir to the Shimada Clan, Hanzo was born the eldest son of the Shimada Clan, a powerful criminal organization in Japan.

From a young age, he was trained in combat, strategy, and leadership.

Expected to one day lead the clan, Hanzo was raised with a strict code of honor and duty.

Unlike his younger brother Genji Shimada, Hanzo fully embraced his responsibilities.

After their father’s death, clan elders ordered Hanzo to bring Genji into line.

When Genji refused the brothers fought in which, Hanzo ultimately defeated Genji, believing he had killed him.

This moment shattered Hanzo, he was consumed by guilt, questioned his loyalty to the clan, and began to reject the life he had been raised for.

Unable to live with what he had done, Hanzo abandoned the Shimada Clan and left behind his position as heir and became a wanderer.

During this time, he honed his skills as an archer taking on mercenary and assassin work as he began seeking atonement for his past.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Hanzo regularly returns to his family’s ancestral home to honor the memory of Genji. During one visit he is confronted by a mysterious cyborg ninja, the two fight, and The ninja reveals himself to be Genji Shimada. Genji forgives Hanzo and tells him to forgive himself.
  • Hanzo possesses the ability to summon spiritual dragon energy, a manifestation tied to the Shimada bloodline.
  • After leaving the clan, Hanzo worked as an assassin and mercenary and took contracts across the world all while avoiding forming close relationships. Despite this, he began choosing missions more carefully and showed starting signs of a developing moral code.
  • Following his reunion with Genji, Hanzo begins actively seeking redemption and struggles to reconcile his past actions in attempt to live by a more honorable path. However, he still carries deep guilt and often isolates himself.
  • In Overwatch 2, Hanzo remains a lone operative being not officially part of Overwatch. He occasionally allies with others when goals align and continues his personal journey toward atonement.
