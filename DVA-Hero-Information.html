Character Summary

D.Va (Hana Song) is a former professional esports champion who now serves as a mech pilot in South Korea’s MEKA defense force. Recruited for her exceptional reflexes, situational awareness, and strategic decision-making, she operates on the front lines against large-scale threats. Publicly, she maintains a high-profile persona shaped by her background as a celebrity gamer, projecting confidence and composure under pressure. In combat, however, she demonstrates discipline, precision, and a strong sense of duty. Her character reflects a balance between media prominence and military professionalism, underscored by a commitment to protecting civilians and national security.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Angela Ziegler was born in Switzerland, likely in the city of Zürich, and was orphaned at a young age due to the Omnic Crisis—a devastating global conflict between humans and omnics (robots with sentient AI). The loss of her parents had a profound impact on Angela, instilling in her a deep hatred of war and a powerful desire to help others through medicine and science rather than violence.

Gifted and brilliant, Angela quickly rose to prominence as a pioneer in nanobiology, particularly in the field of applied nanotechnology. Her research into cellular regeneration was considered revolutionary, and she developed methods that allowed rapid healing of injuries at a molecular level.

Recruitment into Overwatch:

Angela’s talent drew the attention of Overwatch, the international peacekeeping task force formed to combat the Omnic threat. Though she was initially hesitant to join a militarized organization, Angela was persuaded by Overwatch’s humanitarian mission and the opportunity to save lives on a larger scale.

She was given the code name “Mercy” and appointed as the head of medical research. During her time in Overwatch, she designed and wore the Valkyrie Suit, which gave her the ability to fly and use her Caduceus Staff—a powerful healing and damage-boosting device.

Despite her scientific and humanitarian goals, Mercy found herself frequently at odds with some of Overwatch’s more militaristic members (notably Gabriel Reyes, aka Reaper), often arguing that science should be used to save lives, not take them.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • The Valkyrie Suit: Angela’s crowning achievement, the Valkyrie system made her one of the most effective field medics in the world. It allowed her to heal, resurrect fallen comrades, and even enter dangerous zones while gliding to safety.
  • Resurrect Technology: Mercy’s controversial resurrection technology was a medical miracle—but one that raised ethical questions. While reviving the dead was hailed as heroic, Angela herself remained uneasy about the moral implications and the potential misuse of such power.
  • Friendship with Genji: Angela was instrumental in saving Genji Shimada after he was nearly killed by his brother, Hanzo. She was responsible for rebuilding him with cybernetic implants. Genji often sees her as his savior, and the two share a close, potentially romantic, bond—though Mercy has mixed feelings about how much of his humanity was preserved.
  • Overwatch’s Fall: As corruption, infighting, and public distrust grew, Overwatch eventually collapsed. Angela left the organization, disillusioned by its growing militarism and moral decay. Still, she never stopped helping people, continuing her mission in her own way.

Post-Overwatch Activities:

After Overwatch disbanded, Angela adopted a more independent role, operating around the world in crisis zones as a first responder, healer, and humanitarian. She worked in war-torn countries, aided refugees, and provided frontline medical care. No longer officially aligned with any government or military, Mercy chose to serve humanity on her terms.

Return of Overwatch (Overwatch 2 Era):

As new threats emerge—such as the terrorist organization Null Sector and rising omnic rebellions—Mercy is reluctantly drawn back into action. Though she had sworn off combat, the rising global instability convinces her to rejoin the reformed Overwatch under Winston’s leadership. Still true to her values, Mercy serves not as a warrior but as a guardian angel, seeking to heal the world in both body and spirit.
