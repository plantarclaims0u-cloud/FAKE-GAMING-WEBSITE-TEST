Character Summary

D.Va (Hana Song) is a former professional esports champion who now serves as a mech pilot in South Korea’s MEKA defense force.

Recruited for her exceptional reflexes, situational awareness, and strategic decision-making, she operates on the front lines against large-scale threats.

Publicly, she maintains a high-profile persona shaped by her background as a celebrity gamer, projecting confidence and composure under pressure.

In combat, however, she demonstrates discipline, precision, and a strong sense of duty. Her character reflects a balance between media prominence and military professionalism, underscored by a commitment to protecting civilians and national security.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

D.Va (Hana Song) was born in South Korea and rose to fame at a young age as one of the world’s top professional gamers.

Known for her exceptional reflexes, focus, and strategic thinking, she became a global esports champion, earning both prestige and a large public following.

Her life changed when a colossal omnic threat emerged from the sea near South Korea, attacking coastal cities.

In response, the Korean government formed the **Mobile Exo-Force of the Korean Army (MEKA)**, a specialized defense unit equipped with advanced mech technology designed to counter these threats.

Initially, the mechs were operated by traditional control systems, but as the enemy adapted and the attacks intensified, it became clear that human pilots with fast reaction times were necessary.

Due to her elite gaming skills—particularly her hand-eye coordination and ability to process complex situations in real time—Hana Song was recruited into MEKA.

She quickly adapted to piloting the mech, transitioning from virtual combat to real-world battlefield operations.

As a MEKA pilot, D.Va became both a national hero and an international icon.

She continues to defend South Korea from recurring omnic attacks while maintaining her public image as a celebrity.

Despite her playful and confident outward demeanor, she carries the responsibility of protecting millions of lives, balancing the pressures of fame with the demands of active military service.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Defense of South Korea: D.Va plays a central role in protecting South Korea from recurring omnic attacks emerging from the sea. As a MEKA pilot, she directly engages and repels these large-scale threats, helping prevent major destruction and civilian casualties.
  • Recruitment into MEKA: Her transition from professional gamer to soldier is a major turning point. She was selected for the MEKA (Mobile Exo-Force of the Korean Army) program due to her elite reflexes and tactical awareness, marking a shift from virtual competition to real-world combat.
  • Ongoing Omnic Crisis: She participates in repeated defensive operations against evolving omnic forces. These missions require constant adaptation, showcasing her growth as both a pilot and strategist.
  • Rise as a Global Esports Champion: Before her military service, D.Va achieved international fame as a top-tier professional gamer, winning major competitions and building a massive fanbase—this reputation later amplified her public image as a hero.
