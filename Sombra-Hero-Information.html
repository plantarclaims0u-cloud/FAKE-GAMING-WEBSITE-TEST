Character Summary

Sombra (Olivia Colomar) is a cunning, manipulative hacker and infiltrator in Overwatch 2.

She thrives on secrecy, subterfuge, and information control, using her technological skills to manipulate events from behind the scenes.

Sombra is sarcastic, opportunistic, and fiercely independent, often pursuing her own agenda while occasionally collaborating with others for personal gain.

She represents the theme of information as power, showing how knowledge and digital mastery can influence global events.

Backstory

Early Life and Origins:

Sombra grew up in Mexico as an orphan, learning early to survive in a world of scarcity and danger.

She demonstrated exceptional talent for computers and hacking from a young age, quickly mastering the digital landscape.

Her abilities caught the attention of Talon, who covertly manipulated her into working for them while she maintained plausible deniability.

Sombra’s formative experiences taught her the value of secrecy, manipulation, and leverage, making her a master at exploiting weaknesses in both technology and human behavior.

Her past and her skill set drive her to pursue influence and security above all else.

Notable Contributions and Events:
  • Orphaned at a young age and became a self-taught computer prodigy.
  • Developed advanced hacking, infiltration, and cyber-espionage skills.
  • Secretly recruited and manipulated by Talon.
  • Uses cyber attacks to gather intelligence and disrupt targets worldwide.
  • Plays a dual role: both a covert operative for Talon and an independent agent pursuing personal agendas.
  • Leverages secrets, blackmail, and manipulation to influence global politics and criminal networks.
  • Known for destabilizing both organizations and individuals through digital warfare.
